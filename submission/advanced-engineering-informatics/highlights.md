**The Chess Algorithm: A Novel Metaheuristic Optimization Technique with Applications in Transportation Network Engineering**

Seyedsaber Naseralavi, Seyedali Mirjalili

- Chess Algorithm: a metaheuristic with a ranked, role-differentiated population
- A state machine adapts tactics to divergence, initiative and local-search success
- Every mechanism is mapped to a known operator family and ablated in isolation
- Best mean Friedman rank among classical baselines on CEC-2017, CEC-2022, design
- Ablation shows en passant and the knight's fork carry most of the performance
