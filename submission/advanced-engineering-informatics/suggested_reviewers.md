# Suggested reviewers — candidate shortlist

**This file is working notes, not a submission document.** Editorial Manager
asks for name, affiliation, email and a one-line reason per reviewer. The names
below are researchers whose published work sits directly on the topics this
manuscript is judged on. **Affiliations change and no email addresses are filled
in here — look each one up on the researcher's current institutional page before
entering it.** Do not submit a reviewer whose contact details you have not
verified.

Pick three to five. Spread them across the three columns below so the panel
covers the critique, the baselines and the application.

## The metaphor critique and metaheuristic methodology

The manuscript's central claim is methodological — that the chess vocabulary
carries no explanatory weight, because every mechanism is mapped to an
established operator family and ablated. A reviewer from this area tests the
claim that matters most.

- **Kenneth Sörensen** — University of Antwerp, Belgium. Author of "Metaheuristics
  — the metaphor exposed", which the manuscript cites and answers directly.
  A demanding choice, and the honest one.
- **Christian Blum** — IIIA-CSIC, Spain. Hybrid metaheuristics and operator
  taxonomy.
- **Fred Glover / Manuel Laguna** — long-standing work on the component view of
  search methods that the operator-family table relies on.

## Benchmarking, baselines and statistical protocol

The evaluation rests on CEC-2017 and CEC-2022, on L-SHADE and CMA-ES as
baselines, and on Friedman/Wilcoxon testing. These reviewers can check the
protocol and the data-integrity audit.

- **Ponnuthurai Nagaratnam Suganthan** — originator of the CEC benchmark suites
  and their evaluation protocol; best placed to assess the CEC-2017 audit and
  the six restored functions.
- **Ryoji Tanabe** — Yokohama National University, Japan. Co-author of L-SHADE,
  one of the two baselines that outrank CA.
- **Nikolaus Hansen** — Inria, France. Author of CMA-ES, the other.
- **Carlos A. Coello Coello** — CINVESTAV-IPN, Mexico. Constrained optimization
  and the engineering design benchmark set.

## Transportation and engineering application

Sections on arterial signal coordination and berth allocation need a reviewer
who knows the domain models rather than the optimizer.

- **Halim Ceylan** — Pamukkale University, Türkiye. Signal timing optimization by
  metaheuristics; cited in the manuscript.
- **Carlos F. Daganzo / Michael G. H. Bell** — traffic network optimization
  (Bell is a co-author of the cited signal-timing work).
- A container-terminal operations researcher for the berth allocation
  instance — the berth allocation literature (Bierstteker, Vacca, Bierwirth &
  Meisel) is the place to look.
